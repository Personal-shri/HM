Prepared 8 September 2026. Status: feasibility comparison and living-room coordination draft. Existing architectural drawings and 3D geometry are unchanged. The budget route and front compass direction are awaiting the owner's response.

## Budget: the first decision

The existing 3,868 sq ft house is not a confirmed ₹45 lakh project. Its saved preliminary project estimate is ₹111.35–₹149.13 lakh, with exclusions. Retaining those room sizes requires a separate funding decision.

For a smaller redesign, the following arithmetic uses ₹2,100–₹2,600 per sq ft plus the previously proposed ₹11 lakh reserve for exterior upgrades, outside works, fixed interiors, professional fees and contingency. This is a sensitivity check, not a contractor quotation or an approved new floor plan.

| Total area across both floors | Lower-rate total, lakh | Higher-rate total, lakh | Meaning for ₹45 lakh |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1,400 sq ft | 40.40 | 47.40 | Fits only part of the rate range; scope and quotes remain decisive |
| 1,500 sq ft | 42.50 | 50.00 | Little headroom at the low rate; over budget at the high rate |
| 1,600 sq ft | 44.60 | 52.60 | Nearly all headroom consumed at the low rate |

The Latur medium-rate range and scope were rechecked against [RBEA's published construction guide](https://rbeaassociates.com/blog/building-estimate-per-sqft-maharashtra) on 8 September 2026. It is one consultancy's benchmark, not independent local bids. The benchmark includes ordinary building finishes and basic services, but excludes cabinetry, approvals and external development. Do not add those ordinary finishes twice. The source's percentage breakdown is not used.

### Correct the furnishing gap

The previous ₹11 lakh reserve included ₹3 lakh for basic fixed cabinetry, but no loose sofas, beds, dining furniture, TV, fridge or washer. If the full move-in setup must fit ₹45 lakh, those purchases reduce the building allowance. The extra amounts below are illustrative ring-fenced budgets, not researched purchase prices.

| Reserve outside base construction | Remaining base-building allowance | Area at ₹2,600/sq ft | Area at ₹2,100/sq ft |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ₹11 lakh, earlier scope | ₹34 lakh | 1,308 sq ft | 1,619 sq ft |

These area figures are mathematical limits under the chosen assumptions. Compact buildings may cost more per square foot, and site services or soil conditions can reduce the affordable area further. Do not cut the contingency to add furnishings.

### Seating choice to resolve

Option A retains both sofas for conversation plus primary TV seating. Option B replaces the secondary sofa with two movable chairs that can face the TV. Do not add chairs on top of the current layout or presume both sofas provide the same TV comfort. Test a scaled chair alternative after the budget route is decided.

### TV glare and window treatment

The TV stays on the solid left wall, perpendicular to the front glazing. Separate adjustable treatments are proposed for the 8 ft window and 7 ft patio slider. Check curtain parking around the narrow pier and preserve access to the slider. Keep the TV surround matte and avoid bright lamps reflecting toward viewers.

Compass direction, nearby obstructions, sill heights and porch projection are still needed for a daylight study. No claim of glare-free performance is made. Existing room photographs are finish references, not evidence of sunlight or clearance performance.

### Electrical and lighting brief for coordination

- TV zone: concealed power and data route behind the selected screen/console, with accessible equipment connections and serviceable conduit. Heights and socket counts depend on chosen devices and electrician coordination.
- Entry: accessible switching for separately controlled general light and low-level evening light; confirm entry swing first.
- Seating: place any charging provision beside furniture on fixed walls; avoid loose cables crossing the route from entry to hall.
- Ceiling: develop fan and light positions together with beams, curtain tracks and seating. Avoid assigning fixture coordinates before the ceiling layout is known.
- Curtains: determine manual or powered operation before any curtain supply point is specified.
