# Exterior materials and product research

Research checked 8 September 2026. **Current phase: exterior only. Interiors will be planned later.** This study uses the selected ivory classical front and the proposed matching side/rear images. It covers the building envelope, external structural procurement, decorative features and outdoor surfaces. It does not select kitchens, wardrobes, internal flooring, bathroom finishes, furniture or indoor lighting.

## 1. Recommended direction for this house

Keep most of the facade as smooth ivory painted plaster. Concentrate the decorative work on the entrance arch, a few cornice bands and window surrounds. Use simple dark metal railings, carefully proportioned dark window frames and limited warm exterior lighting. Choose a small coordinated palette rather than many different finishes. These are design recommendations based on the selected image, not manufacturer instructions.

The image does not identify exact products, paint codes, hidden waterproofing or structural details. The creamy appearance can be sampled; it cannot be reliably colour-matched from an image under warm evening light. View physical ivory samples in daylight, shade and at night with the intended lamps. Retain one approved sample panel throughout construction.

The ₹45 lakh ceiling remains for the whole project. Product substitutions cannot make the unchanged 3,868 sq ft house fit it. The previous ₹1.5 lakh decorative-exterior allocation applies only to a compact redesign and excludes ordinary external paint, windows, structural works and roof waterproofing already allocated elsewhere. This research adds no new budget approvals and changes no floor plan.

## 2. Evidence and buying status

Manufacturer pages and technical data sheets are the primary sources. Named products below are candidates for comparison, not confirmed orders. Manufacturer performance and warranty statements are claims subject to their stated application conditions. Published MRP is not an installed quotation. Local stock, delivery to the plot, colour surcharges, contractor labour and warranty eligibility have not been confirmed. No supplier has been contacted and no enquiry form has been submitted.

## 3. Exterior walls, masonry and plaster

**Compare two complete installed wall systems:** locally supplied compliant brickwork with cement plaster, versus AAC blocks with their specified jointing mortar and compatible render. Decide on delivered wall cost and installer competence, not block price alone. The engineer must approve wall thickness, structural implications, junctions and opening supports.

Magicrete publishes an AAC system with purpose-made thin-bed mortar. Its technical sheet also addresses moisture protection, copings, sills and frame sealing. That makes it a useful system to quote; it does not establish that AAC is cheaper or available near this plot. [Magicrete AAC technical sheet](https://www.magicrete.in/assets/uploads/2026/02/productknowledge/aac-blocks-tds_1772089153.pdf), [block jointing mortar](https://www.magicrete.in/walling-solutions/dry-mix-products/block-jointing-mortar).

UltraTech Readiplast is a cement-based ready-mix render candidate for external brick/block walls. Compare it with a controlled site-mixed cement render on installed cost and finish quality. The linked manufacturer's sheet is older: obtain the current Readiplast / Readiplast Pro sheet for the supplied product and follow that curing procedure. Do not assume similarly named versions have identical instructions. [UltraTech render sheet](https://www.ultratechcement.com/content/dam/ultratechcement/pdf/plastering-material.pdf).

Quote wall area/volume, thickness, mortar, transport, scaffold, junction mesh where specified, opening deductions, plaster layers, curing and surface preparation separately. Avoid general interior gypsum products on rain-exposed surfaces. Cornices must not conceal unresolved cracks or wall-frame movement.

## 4. Ivory exterior paint: value and premium options

**Value candidate: Asian Paints Apex Dust Proof Emulsion.** Its official page lists a 20 L MRP of ₹8,510 including taxes, with shade/finish variation noted. This is a sensible candidate for a value quotation over properly prepared external plaster. Confirm the currently supplied variant, compatible primer and declared finish before comparing costs. [Product and published price](https://www.asianpaints.com/paint-products/exterior-wall-paints/apex-dustproof-emulsion.html).

**Premium comparison: Asian Paints Apex Ultima Protek.** The page lists ₹13,840 for 20 L, tax-inclusive, and a conditional 12-year paint-performance warranty plus 10-year waterproofing/anti-algal terms. It is for vertical external walls. Basecoat versus primer affects the warranty; the page states a minimum 40 L topcoat purchase with the respective undercoat for warranty eligibility. Do not buy extra material solely for a longer headline warranty. [Product, price and conditions](https://www.asianpaints.com/paint-products/exterior-wall-paints/apex-ultima-protek.html).

**Alternative quotation: Berger Weathercoat Long Life 10.** The official page describes a premium PU/silicon exterior coating. Its body mentions 10 years while a benefit badge says 12 years. Record the written warranty supplied with the exact system instead of choosing either number as settled. Price requires a local quote. [Berger product page](https://www.bergerpaints.com/products/exterior-wall-coatings/weathercoat-long-life-10).

Request one consistent paint system including substrate repairs, primer/basecoat, finish coats, required consumption and registration. A premium topcoat does not fix bad plaster or roof leakage. Ask whether the offered sheen suits the restrained image; glossy products may emphasize uneven surfaces. Put a separate price on the premium upgrade so it can be removed without reducing preparation quality.

## 5. Cornices, window surrounds, arch and cladding

**First quotation:** modest site-formed cement-render profiles on a properly detailed substrate. Keep a repeated profile family and short projections. This is our cost-conscious design direction, not a verified lowest-price claim. Have the designer specify support, weathering, drips and movement joints.

**Second quotation:** factory-made GRC/GFRC decorative pieces for repeat cornices or the entrance. GRC is glass-fibre reinforced cement/concrete; it is different from plaster-of-Paris or fibreglass plastic decoration. Require shop drawings, product test evidence, component weights, lifting/installation method and engineer-approved anchors. The GRCA describes specification and testing guidance, but the older practical guide could not be retrieved in this research; no local GRC maker is approved here. [GRCA publication background](https://grca.org.uk/grc-news/grc-book-from-grca.php).

Avoid choosing generic foam or interior POP profiles just because they reproduce the shape cheaply. An exterior lightweight profile would need a documented complete coating/fixing system and project-specific fire, weathering and impact review. No such product has been verified for this project.

For the small entrance accent, compare textured plaster with a limited beige exterior tile/stone strip. Full stone cladding is not needed to reproduce the selected ivory character. Heavy stone at height requires a designed attachment system; tile adhesive alone is not a universal anchoring solution.

For suitable tile applications, SikaCeram-255 Large Tile is an Indian exterior-capable adhesive candidate, identified as C2TE / Type 3 T on the manufacturer's page. This is not blanket approval for every stone, height or substrate: confirm the complete assembly and whether mechanical restraint is required. [SikaCeram-255](https://ind.sika.com/en/construction/tile-installationsystems/tile-adhesives/sikaceram-255-largetile.html).

## 6. Flat roofs, balcony and terrace waterproofing

The concealed flat roof is a defining part of the selected appearance. Keep it visually flat behind the parapet while designing the actual waterproofing substrate and drainage falls. Provide accessible outlets, appropriate overflow provisions, sealed penetrations and weathered parapet tops. Levels and falls must be coordinated before door thresholds and paving are fixed.

**Tiled balcony/terrace candidate:** SikaTop-107 Seal Plus IN, a two-part cementitious waterproofing system whose India page lists terraces and balconies. Published consumption is about 1.5–2.0 kg/m² in two coats, with substrate-dependent variation. The page describes a protective layer and says not to pond before seven days. The full substrate, reinforcement/detailing, curing and protection specification must be obtained from the applicator. [India product data](https://ind.sika.com/en/construction/waterproofing-systems/waterproofing-mortar/sikatop-107-sealplusin.html).

**Exposed roof comparison:** Dr. Fixit Roofseal Classic, a PU-acrylic roof-coating candidate. Its official page contains conflicting seven- and ten-year warranty statements. Its visible ₹465 price is not used for budgeting because pack selection is ambiguous in the retrieved page. Obtain the exact pack quotation, consumption, traffic limitations and signed warranty terms. [Roofseal Classic](https://www.drfixit.co.in/products/dr-fixit-roofseal-classic/detail).

These systems are alternatives for different exposure and finish conditions, not products to stack together without approval. An exposed coating should not automatically be treated as the wearing surface below chairs and tables. A water-resistant wall paint or a concrete additive is not a substitute for a coordinated terrace system. Price the slope layer, membrane, upstands, outlets, testing and protection separately; keep RCC slab cost in the structural package.

## 7. Windows, balcony doors and glass

**Preferred comparison format:** one capable local powder-coated aluminium fabricator versus a documented system supplier. Ask both to quote the same opening schedule and performance requirements. Dark aluminium is a visual match candidate; uPVC is a possible alternative only after comparing frame sightlines, available dark finishes and exposure suitability.

Fenesta offers aluminium sliding/casement and other window systems, with selection depending on aperture size, wind load, insulation and price. Use it as a performance and service benchmark, not an assumed budget winner. Large openings require a suitable engineered series, not the thinnest section that resembles the photo. [Fenesta aluminium systems](https://www.fenesta.com/window/aluminium-windows).

Specify the opening type, fixed/openable split, ventilation provision, insect mesh, hardware, coating, drainage paths, glass build-up, perimeter seal and installation warranty. The view through the window and the thickness of visible frames materially change the facade. Full-house supplier price per sq ft without these details is not a comparable quote.

**Glass decision:** compare neutral clear/safety glass and appropriate solar-control options using actual visible-light transmission and solar factor. Saint-Gobain's Sun Ban table, for example, lists Black Diamond at only 10.6% visible transmission in the published 5 mm configuration. That is a reason not to order dark reflective glass automatically when daylight and clear-looking windows are priorities. It is not a recommendation to use 5 mm glass on this house. [Sun Ban performance table](https://in.saint-gobain-glass.com/sun-ban-sgg).

Glass thickness, toughening/lamination and fall-risk locations require the supplier/designer's safety assessment. Solar control, impact safety and insulation are different specifications. Road-facing compass orientation is still unknown, so no elevation-specific glazing package is fixed.

## 8. Window seals and concealed water details

Require a compatible exterior perimeter sealant with joint geometry, backing material and surface preparation specified by the window supplier. Sika Sikasil WS-605 S is a weather-sealing reference for windows/facades; its global product page is not confirmation of current Indian dealer stock or suitability for every frame/stone interface. Ask for local technical approval and compatibility testing. It is not specified here as a structural glazing adhesive. [Sika weather sealant](https://industry.sika.com/en/home/building-components/facade/weather-sealing/sikasil-ws-605-s.html).

Make window sills, jamb returns, parapet copings and cornice drip edges part of the drawings and the quotation. Keep intentional frame drainage openings clear. These details are less visible in the image but essential to keeping ivory walls free of recurrent water staining.

Tata Pravesh is a candidate to investigate for steel/entrance doors. The official lead page identifies these categories, but the main site returned an access error during research. Obtain a catalogue and a written drawing of the proposed assembly before listing it as a selected product. [Official Tata Pravesh enquiry/catalogue route](https://lead.tatapravesh.com/).

## 10. Dark metal balcony and terrace railings

Use the straight vertical-bar pattern from the selected image. Compare a properly prepared painted steel assembly with a galvanized-and-coated alternative where available. Obtain actual installed prices: galvanizing logistics and minimum job charges may affect a small order. Stainless steel or glass railings are not necessary to preserve this design.

Asian Paints Apcolite Premium Gloss Enamel is a named metal-finish comparison, with a technical sheet distinguishing primers by substrate. A steel coating specification cannot simply be copied onto galvanized metal or aluminium. If the gloss is too shiny for the reference, ask for a compatible lower-sheen alternative with documented exterior use. [Metal coating technical sheet](https://www.asianpaints.com/content/dam/asian_paints/products/product-information-sheets/apcolite-premium-gloss-enamel-pis-new.pdf).

Final guard height, gaps, member sizes, loading and anchorage require the architect/engineer and applicable local requirements. Do not use decorative GRC posts or parapet caps as unverified structural anchors. Detail penetrations without compromising terrace waterproofing. The quote should include weld dressing, surface preparation, corrosion protection, site touch-up and fixings.

## 11. Exterior lighting only

Our design direction is warm light around 2700–3000 K, a restrained number of lantern-style entry fittings, shielded side/rear utility lights and safe step illumination. Colour temperature is a design preference, not a verified property of every product below. Avoid a brightly floodlit front if the goal is the soft selected appearance.

**Verified comparison:** Philips outdoor wall light 919215100070 is listed as 7 W, grey aluminium, IP44, with MRP ₹4,550. It is a modern bidirectional fitting rather than an exact lantern match. Consider only for a suitably sheltered location approved by the installer; do not treat IP44 as suitable for every rain-exposed position. [Philips product](https://www.lighting.philips.co.in/consumer/p/led-outdoor-wall-light/919215100070).

**Utility-light comparison:** Wipro's Garnet floodlight listing shows ₹1,399 against ₹2,250 and is marked sold out; the listing references IP66. The variant/wattage is not fixed by that category price. It is an availability/price lead, not a procurement line. [Wipro listing](https://www.wiproconsumerlighting.com/products/outdoor-lights/floodlights).

Crompton's outdoor range includes lantern/gate-light families, a closer appearance lead for the entrance. The category claims IP65; confirm the exact fixture's datasheet, mounting type and warm-white option rather than applying a family claim to every model. [Crompton outdoor range](https://www.crompton.co.in/collections/outdoor-lights).

For exposed wall fixtures, ask the electrician to assess at least IP65 candidates and suitable cable glands/junction boxes; installation and location still matter. No light listed here is approved for immersion. Keep decorative fittings separate from wiring, protection and earthing costs. Six of the Philips reference fixtures alone would be ₹27,300 at listed MRP, before installation—above the earlier compact ₹15,000 decorative-light allowance. Quote fewer fixtures or a less costly verified outdoor product instead.

## 12. Patios, steps and terrace floor finishes

Compare textured local stone, concrete pavers for suitable ground-level areas, and exterior-rated vitrified/porcelain tiles. Keep pale beige/grey tones near the ivory facade and test the actual surface wet. A matte label alone does not prove slip performance. Material thickness and substrate must match pedestrian or vehicle loading; do not use a ground paver build-up on a balcony without checking its weight.

Kajaria DuRock and Duratech/X-Stone are range candidates. The X-Stone catalogue identifies R11 surfaces and includes 20 mm pavers. That does not mean every tile in every Kajaria range is R11 or 20 mm, nor is R11 a guarantee against slipping under all conditions. Obtain the chosen SKU's test data and intended laying method. [Kajaria outdoor range](https://www.kajariaceramics.com/outdoor-tiles), [X-Stone catalogue](https://www.kajariaceramics.com/catalogo/catalogue/duratech-xstone.pdf).

Separate ground patio layers from upper-terrace protection/screed. Include edge/nosing pieces, compatible bedding/adhesive, grout, movement joints and drainage. Large-format tile does not remove the need for joints. Use sealed, accessible outlets and prevent water from draining toward entrance thresholds.

## 13. Rainwater, landscape edges and outside services

For the flat roof, ask for a coordinated roof-outlet/downpipe design rather than selecting pipe diameter from the photo. Astral supplies drainage product families, but its Rainway brochure describes an eaves/roof collection system; it is not automatically the right system for a concealed flat-roof parapet. Match products to the drainage designer's arrangement. [Astral drainage catalogue overview](https://www.astralpipes.com/), [Rainway brochure](https://www.astralpipes.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Rainway.pdf).

Route downpipes and service penetrations early so they do not interrupt the chosen front. Allow access for clearing blockages. Final discharge/recharge arrangements depend on the site and local approvals. No borewell, septic system or full boundary package is priced by this research.

Treat the lush garden, large pots and outdoor furniture in the render as optional items. Start with modest planting and a simple paved entrance after drainage is complete. Defer premium furniture, extensive lighting and elaborate landscape edging before cutting weatherproofing or guardrails. Terrace planters need load and waterproofing review.

## 14. RCC, steel and foundations: procurement boundaries

The frame supports the exterior but cannot be designed from product brochures. Footing geometry, column dimensions, slab thickness, concrete grade and reinforcement must follow the soil report and engineer-approved drawings. The outside image does not establish any of those quantities.

UltraTech Concrete is a ready-mix supplier candidate with an official plant locator. Compare engineered ready-mix supply against a controlled site-batched proposal only after checking local availability, truck/pump access, small-load charges, testing and pour logistics. No claim of a Latur-serving plant has been verified. [UltraTech Concrete](https://www.ultratechcement.com/for-homebuilders/products/ultratech-concrete).

Tata Tiscon's official site provides rebar products, recommended consumer price and dealer routes. Its 550SD product name is not permission to replace a grade specified by the engineer. Require approved grade, bar marking, manufacturer test documents, quantity reconciliation and a clear delivered-price basis. No project steel tonnage or rupees/kg quotation is established here. [Tata Tiscon](https://tatatiscon.co.in/).

Keep concrete, reinforcement and formwork distinct in the quantity bill, or explicitly define what a composite RCC rate includes. Foundation and above-plinth RCC must not be counted twice. Avoid higher-strength-brand marketing as a substitute for correct detailing, placing, curing and inspection.

## 17. Construction sequence and maintenance handover

Approve dimensioned elevations and structural/service coordination first. Next, complete wall/opening surveys, door/window shop drawings and a material sample panel. Fix external drainage, penetrations and supporting/anchoring provisions before closing up finishes. Install compatible waterproofing and protection to the approved sequence, with documented inspection/testing before covering it. Complete window seals, profiles, coatings and outdoor lighting with protection for finished surfaces.

At handover, retain approved drawings, product invoices, batch/colour codes, technical sheets, warranty registration, test records, drainage photographs, cleaning instructions and installer contacts. Inspect after the first monsoon, and arrange periodic checks for blocked outlets, cracked seals, loose fittings, coating damage and railing corrosion. This inspection suggestion is not a substitute for each manufacturer's maintenance terms.
