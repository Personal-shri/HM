# Main-floor interiors — furniture and layout proposal v1

Scope: main/ground floor only. Uses the current 44 × 48 ft schematic plan, approximately 2,112 sq ft on this floor. Upper-floor interiors are deferred. The ₹45 lakh feasibility review remains separate: this is NOT a compact redesign and does not establish affordability of the existing full-sized house.

## How to use this proposal

Room sizes on the plan are planning zones, not verified finished dimensions. Furniture dimensions below are proposed overall footprints, not shopping specifications. Nominal mattress size is different from the bed-frame footprint. Final wall thickness, door swings, window heights, columns, cabinet handles and equipment clearances must be included before ordering. Clearances here are design targets, not claims of code compliance or universal accessibility.

Three rendered views show the living room, main kitchen and parents' bedroom. They illustrate finishes and atmosphere; the dimensioned plan and the proposed schedule below take precedence. Known render differences: the living image relocates a large window onto an adjacent wall and its seating needs further adjustment to preserve slider access; the kitchen image adds a left-side door that is not established by the plan, and its island countertop differs from the preferred economical furniture-table option. Do not add those openings to the building. No interior 3D walkthrough is included yet.

## Shared design direction

Warm ivory walls, light beige matte tile, cream and wood-tone laminate cabinetry, dark window frames and small muted-green accents connect the inside with the chosen exterior. Use plain ceilings and carefully placed basic lighting/fans as the starting point. Decorative false ceilings, full wall panelling and premium stone are optional later items, not base requirements.

## Kitchen: test the working space before ordering

Use a 6 × 3 ft worktable/island to start; the earlier plan's 8 × 4 ft furniture symbol is not a fixed requirement. No sink or hob in the island initially, simplifying services. Choose the appliance models before cabinet fabrication.

A nominal front-to-back fit check in the 14 ft kitchen zone is: 2 ft counter + 3 ft 6 in working aisle + 3 ft island + 4 ft 6 in dining-facing clearance = 13 ft. This leaves only 1 ft of nominal allowance for wall/position adjustments; it is not proof of a finished fit. On the stool side, measure remaining walking space with stools occupied, not tucked in. If clearances fail after measuring walls and fridge/oven openings, shrink or omit the island instead of narrowing the passage.

Start the counter specification around 2 ft depth and 2 ft 10 in to 3 ft height, then adapt to the users and appliance requirements. Sink bowl, hob, chimney, fridge ventilation, opening doors, gas provision and electrical connections must follow the selected products' requirements. Do not place a hob immediately against a window or route fan airflow across it. The fan and hanging lights shown in the concept need a coordinated ceiling layout.

Keep the pantry accessible from dining. Use a simple stone worktop and laminate cabinetry as quotation options; the choice of board, edge sealing and wet-zone construction must be explicit. Do not equate a cream finish with a particular durable material specification.

## Living room: preserve seating and the entrance route

The sofas should face the TV and each other, with approximately 16–18 in between seating and coffee table as an initial reach target. Keep a continuous roughly 3 ft 6 in to 4 ft circulation route on the foyer/right-hand side where geometry permits, leading toward the family hall. Slider operation and curtain stack need clear space. Final sofa positions must be checked on a scaled furnished drawing with the actual door/window locations; the render is not that drawing.

Choose TV size after establishing actual seating distance and comfortable viewing angles. Reserve power/data at the TV wall and convenient switched sockets beside seating; avoid extension cables across routes. A small rug is optional and should not curl or intrude into the primary walking path.

## Parents' room: comfort before decoration

Use a comfortable bed height selected with the parents, a supportive chair with arms, reachable bedside switches and an uncluttered route to the bathroom. Keep the closet as the principal clothes storage instead of adding a second full wardrobe in the bedroom. The 6 × 6 closet requires particular care because its nominal dimensions shrink after wall finishes. These choices are comfort intentions, not a verified accessible layout.

## Laundry and store: preserve the agreed connection

Confirm washer dimensions including hoses, vibration clearance and open door. With an approximately 2 ft 6 in deep appliance, a 6 ft nominal room leaves only 3 ft 6 in before wall deductions. Avoid a second deep cabinet bank opposite. Shelves may sit above equipment only with safe access and clear window operation. The store's sliding door must have unobstructed leaf travel on the store side; a shelf must not occupy that travel zone.

## Planning the rest of the main floor

Use the same floor tile and wall palette across living, hall and dining for continuity. Bathrooms and laundry need suitable wet-area flooring, waterproofing, drainage and ventilation. Mark sockets, lights, fan positions, plumbing points and curtain provisions after the furniture layout is agreed, before site conduits and pipes are installed. Do not conceal unresolved columns or service shafts behind render-only furniture.

## Budget relationship

The compact-budget study allows ₹3 lakh for basic fixed interiors only; it does not fund all the furniture, appliances, curtains, lights and decor visible in these images for the current house. Flooring, basic services and standard paint have separate construction allowances. Request separate quotes for fixed cabinetry, loose furniture, appliances and decorative lighting so the same work is not counted twice. No new price quotation is asserted in this interior concept.
