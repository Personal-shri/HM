# ₹45 lakh budget — selected exterior and construction scope

Prepared 8 September 2026. Location: near Latur, Maharashtra. Land is owned. The ₹45 lakh ceiling includes construction, basic fixed interiors and approvals. The selected ivory classical exterior remains the appearance reference. The existing 3,868 sq ft plan has not been reduced or changed.

## Decision in plain language

The exact existing house, with its present area, large rooms, two floors, glazing and outdoor spaces, is not supported by a ₹45 lakh budget. We can investigate retaining its exterior character in a smaller building, but that changes dimensions and accommodation. Neither a cheaper facade nor reduced decoration closes the area-driven funding gap. Do not economize by reducing engineered foundations or reinforcement.

## Cost check for the existing house

₹45,00,000 / 3,868 sq ft = approximately ₹1,163 per sq ft, with nothing reserved for approvals, cabinetry, outdoor works or contingency.

A published Latur benchmark lists basic construction at ₹1,700–₹2,100 per sq ft and medium construction at ₹2,100–₹2,600. On the saved area, that implies approximately ₹65.8–₹81.2 lakh for the basic building alone, or ₹81.2–₹100.6 lakh at the medium benchmark. These are indicative market benchmarks, not bids for this house. The saved full-project estimate remains roughly ₹1.1–₹1.5 crore with its stated exclusions; the selected decorative details have not been separately quoted.

Source checked: [RBEA construction guide](https://rbeaassociates.com/blog/building-estimate-per-sqft-maharashtra). Its scope includes standard openings, floors, basic services, paint and kitchen platforms; cabinetry, approvals and external development are outside that rate. Do not use its percentage breakdown as an additive bill: the listed percentages overlap or exceed 100%.

## Footing lengths, columns and quantities: what is still needed

A photograph cannot establish soil bearing capacity, loads, spans, foundation type, excavation depth, column sizes, bar diameters or reinforcement spacing. The current drawing does not contain an engineer-approved structural grid. No footing count, footing length/width/depth, column count, column size, slab thickness, concrete grade or steel weight is fixed by this budget.

The architect and structural engineer should first coordinate the architectural plan, site levels, geotechnical report and load paths. Open living spaces and balcony supports must be resolved before finalizing the frame. Soil conditions could require a different foundation system, with a significant cost effect.

| Item | Measurement for the eventual quantity bill | Required information |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Excavation | Sum of pit or trench length × width × depth, m³ | Foundation drawings, safe working space, soil and disposal conditions |
| PCC below foundations | Sum of length × width × thickness, m³ | Approved base dimensions and concrete specification |
| Footing RCC | Sum of actual designed footing volumes, m³ | Stepped/sloped/combined geometry; not one invented standard pad |
| Pedestals and columns | Sum of cross-section × measured height, m³ | Column schedule and level boundaries; prevent beam/slab overlap |
| Plinth and floor beams | Net beam volumes under agreed measurement rules, m³ | Beam schedule and junction deductions |
| Floor, roof and stair RCC | Designed net concrete volume, m³ | Openings, drops, landings, waist slabs and balcony details |
| Reinforcement | Engineer's bar-bending schedule, kg | Bar lengths, bends, laps, anchorage and permitted waste separately stated |
| Formwork | Actual concrete-contact surface area, m² | Each member's geometry; avoid charging hidden contact faces |
| Walls | Length × height × thickness minus applicable deductions, m³ | Wall layout, thickness, openings and chosen masonry system |
| Plaster, paint and waterproofing | Actual treated surface area, m² | Internal/external faces, openings, returns and upstands |
| Decorative profiles and railings | Running metres / m² with cross-section or height | Dimensioned elevations, fixings and finish specification |
| Windows and doors | Opening schedule by type, size and count | Frame section, glass, hardware, mesh, installation and sealing |

These are quantity-taking methods, not structural design instructions. Use a consistent measurement convention and convert feet to metres before calculating a metric bill.

## Quality that should remain protected

Require engineer-approved reinforcement and concrete specifications, documented material supply, correct placement and cover, curing and prescribed tests. Do not accept a contractor's economy proposal that changes structural members without the engineer's approval.

Waterproofing, roof slopes, drip details and window sealing affect both appearance and durability. Keep their specifications clear. Simplify ornament and furniture before cutting these works.

Compare local brick and block options on the complete installed wall: material, freight, laying, plaster, junction detailing and wastage. Align service spaces where the revised plan permits. Repeat window sizes and decorative profiles where compatible with light, ventilation and the selected appearance. These are options to quote, not guaranteed savings.

## Obtain comparable quotations

Ask three local contractors to price the same architectural drawings, structural drawings and quantity schedule. Separate concrete, steel and formwork, or explicitly confirm that a composite RCC rate includes all three. Require material specifications, quantity, unit rate, amount, taxes, freight, waste, access/scaffolding, testing and exclusions. Do not place a material order using this budget's allowances.

The official [Maharashtra PWD schedule page](https://pwd.maharashtra.gov.in/en/publication/state-schedule-of-rate-2022-2023/) currently links a schedule titled 2022–2023, even though its publication metadata says 2025. It is a useful item-description reference, not proof of September 2026 Latur contractor prices. Current project rates and applicable adjustments need local verification.

## Scope exclusions and next decision

No loose furniture, appliances, AC, solar, garage, full boundary/gate package, extensive garden, borewell or substantial septic/sewer works is funded here. If any is required within ₹45 lakh, reserve it before choosing the building area. Unusual soil/foundations, rock excavation and major service infrastructure are unresolved risks, not included promises.

Next design decision: either commission a compact two-story redesign that retains the selected exterior character, or retain the current exact-sized design and arrange a larger budget. A phased build is a separate engineered project; ₹45 lakh should not be assumed to complete the current 2,112 sq ft main floor with foundations for the future upper floor. The current approved appearance and plan remain unchanged pending that choice.
