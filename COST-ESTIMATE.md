## Doors and large windows — included versus extra

The drawing has 596 sq ft of window/vent openings, plus two 7 × 8 ft glazed sliding doors. Their dimensions are nominal openings, not clear escape dimensions.

- **Windows/vents:** 596 sq ft × ₹650–₹1,200 gives about **₹3.9–₹7.2 lakh**. The rate is a Pune supplier's installed mid-range benchmark used only as a Maharashtra proxy; local fabrication, glass, mesh and tax scope must be confirmed. [Hakimi window guide](https://pune.hakimiglass.com/blog/aluminium-sliding-windows-price-pune/).
- **All doors, including two glazed sliders:** allow approximately **₹3.5–₹6.5 lakh**, depending on main-door material, shutters, frames, glazing, hardware and fitting. This is our provisional package allowance, not a measured supplier quote. Door price context: [Studio Matrx door-cost guide](https://www.studiomatrx.org/guides/door-cost-india-2026).
- **Do not add both full packages to the base building rate.** That rate already includes standard openings. The table only adds a provisional ₹3–₹6 lakh upgrade difference. Reconcile the actual opening quotation against the contractor's included allowance before fixing the total.

## What changed from the original ₹45 lakh discussion

The initial house was roughly 1,800 sq ft with economical finishes. The current American-style design is roughly 3,868 sq ft, with five bathrooms, two kitchens, larger rooms and extensive glazing/outdoor areas. It is a substantially different project and is not expected to fit ₹45 lakh.

## Before obtaining a fixed price

Confirm the site and soil conditions, wall thickness, structural spans beneath the upstairs bedrooms, balcony support, stair rise/run and openings, actual room clearances and fixtures, window orientation and shading, glazing specification, and quantity schedule. Large window assemblies may combine fixed and operable panels; they are not necessarily single moving panes. Window sill/head heights and bedroom escape openings remain subject to design review.
