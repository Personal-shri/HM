## Daylight and ventilation intent

- Use large multi-panel windows with a mix of fixed and openable sections; final glass design is not specified.
- Kitchen windows should align with counter and cabinet heights; nominal 4 ft window height is a starting point.
- Review orientation and shading before choosing glass: more glass also changes heat and glare.
- Keep the proposed windows clear of tall wardrobes and furniture. Internal bathrooms need designed mechanical exhaust or a revised ventilation shaft.
- Bedroom escape openings, safety glazing, exterior setbacks and structural lintels must be verified locally.
