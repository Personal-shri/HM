Walk through the main floor

Drag to look around. Use W/A/S/D, arrow keys or the on-screen buttons to move. Choose a room to jump there.

Scaled interior exploration, not a photoreal or construction model. Walls and openings come from the saved main-floor drawing; furniture comes from revision 03. Wall thickness (about 4 in), wall height (9.5 ft), eye height (5.2 ft) and window sill heights are visualization assumptions. Doors are shown open as clear passages; door-leaf swings still need coordination. The ceiling is omitted for the overhead view. Bathrooms are empty planning zones; stairs are reserved and cannot be climbed. Glare is not simulated. Some tight passages need room shortcuts until architectural clearances are resolved.

If movement seems blocked, release the controls, choose a room or return to the entrance. This single HTML file works offline in a browser with WebGL.
