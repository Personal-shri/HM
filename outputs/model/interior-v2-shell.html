Start with the overhead model. Choose a room to walk inside, inspect its furniture or compare its saved photo sample.

Version 2: detailed furniture and saved photos, with unchanged scaled room geometry. Walls and openings come from the saved main-floor drawing; furniture footprints come from revision 03; cushions, cabinetry, appliances and finishes are visualization details. Photos are references beside the model, not reconstructed 3D textures. Wall thickness (about 4 in), wall height (9.5 ft), eye height (5.2 ft) and window sill heights are visualization assumptions. Doors are shown open as clear passages; door-leaf swings still need coordination. The ceiling is omitted for the overhead view. Bathrooms are empty planning zones; stairs are reserved and cannot be climbed. Glare is not simulated. Some tight passages need room shortcuts until architectural clearances are resolved.

If movement seems blocked, release the controls, choose a room or return to the entrance. This single HTML file works offline in a browser with WebGL.
