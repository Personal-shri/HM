01 / GATHER TOGETHER

The great room.

24 × 20 ft overall planning zone, including the 6 × 6 ft foyer. Two sofas form a conversation area while the entrance route stays open.

Furniture starting sizes

  • Main sofa: 7 ft 6 in × 3 ft
  • Second sofa: 6 × 3 ft
  • Coffee table: 3 ft 6 in × 2 ft
  • TV console: 5 ft × 1 ft 3 in

Keep the route clear

Target a 3 ft 6 in–4 ft route from foyer toward the family hall. Keep the patio slider usable and allow room for curtains. The render changes window placement; the saved floor plan governs the actual openings.

02 / EVERYDAY COOKING

The main kitchen & dining.

20 × 14 ft kitchen zone with dining behind. Cream cabinets, a practical worktop and a smaller island keep the layout simple.

Counter & furniture sizes

  • Perimeter counters: nominal 2 ft deep
  • Island/worktable: 6 × 3 ft, two stools
  • Dining table: 6 × 3 ft, six seats
  • Fridge planning space: about 3 × 3 ft; verify model

Check before building

Start with 3 ft 6 in working space between front counter and island, and 4 ft 6 in on the dining side. Check occupied stools and opening appliances. Shrink or omit the island if the measured fit fails. The extra left-side door in this render is not part of the floor plan.

03 / REST & COMFORT

Your parents’ room.

16 × 16 ft planning zone at the rear-left, with separate ensuite and closet access. A comfortable bed, supportive chair and uncluttered routes come first.

Furniture starting sizes

  • Queen mattress: 5 ft × 6 ft 6 in
  • Allow bed frame: about 5 ft 6 in × 7 ft
  • Bedside tables: 1 ft 6 in square
  • Armchair: about 2 ft 6 in square

Easy daily use

Target 3 ft clear routes to the bed and bathroom. Keep both rear doors accessible. In the 6 × 6 ft closet, start with one 2 ft deep hanging bank; do not place two deep banks opposite each other without a clearance check.
