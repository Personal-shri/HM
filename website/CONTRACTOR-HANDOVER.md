# HM — contractor and professional handover templates

Prepared 8 September 2026. Draft templates only. Nothing has been issued to a supplier. Complete all blanks and attach coordinated drawings before requesting a firm price. Project: near Latur; 55 × 80 ft plot; ₹45 lakh all-in target excluding owned land. Exact locality and authority remain to be confirmed.

## 1. Site information request

- Village/locality and survey/property number: pending.
- Planning authority and land-use status: local architect to confirm.
- Boundary survey, north arrow, road width, approach and levels: surveyor to provide.
- Water, drainage/sewer or on-site treatment, power and construction access: verify.
- Geotechnical investigation: engineer to define scope based on building loads.
- Applicable setbacks, coverage, floor area, height and required permissions: written confirmation from local architect/authority.

## 2. Request for quotation — complete before issue

Package: ______
Drawing numbers, revisions and date: ______
Specification and approved equivalents: ______
Measured quantity schedule attached: ______
Scope inclusions and exclusions: ______
Site access and storage constraints: ______
Quote due date and required validity: ______

Please itemize quantity, unit, material rate, labour, freight, unloading, access/scaffolding, waste, testing, tax and total. Identify any composite rates to prevent double counting. State substitutions separately with supporting product data. Include delivery lead time, installation duration, warranty conditions, payment milestones and excluded work. Do not silently replace the specified system.

For RCC: explicitly state whether concrete, reinforcement and formwork are priced separately or included in a composite rate. Reinforcement quantities must come from checked schedules. Do not infer member sizes from architectural images.

For openings: include profile system, finish, glass specification, hardware, mesh if required, perimeter sealing, fixings, installation and transport. Use unique opening IDs and drawing revisions.

For waterproofing: state substrate preparation, slope/drain responsibilities, system and consumption, upstands, penetrations, protection, testing, labour and warranty exclusions.

For exterior finish: separate ordinary plaster/paint from decorative profiles, railing, feature cladding and decorative lights already allocated elsewhere.

## 3. Inspection and order sequence

| Stage | Required predecessor | Hold point / evidence | Ordering action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Site setup | Survey, permissions and coordinated construction issue | Boundaries and levels checked | Plan access, storage and temporary services |
| Foundations | Geotechnical and structural design | Engineer reviews excavation conditions, reinforcement and formwork before pours | Order by approved quantities and pour schedule |
| Frame and stairs | Approved framing and service openings | Reinforcement, formwork, embedded items; prescribed concrete tests and curing records | Stage steel/concrete deliveries; verify stock and quantities |
| Walls and services | Frame release and coordinated layouts | Check opening positions and concealed services before covering | Confirm masonry and opening sizes; order within supplier lead time |
| Roof and envelope | Falls, drains and junction details | Agreed waterproofing tests before protection; inspect window seals | Approve complete compatible system and sample |
| Finishes and fixed interiors | Dry, accepted substrate and tested concealed services | Compare with signed samples; protect finished work | Release quantities after final measurement |
| Handover | Work substantially complete | Service tests, defects list, as-built records and warranties | Close purchase and payment records against contract |

Dates, test methods, duration, acceptance criteria and contractual payment percentages are to be agreed with the contractor and responsible professionals. This is a dependency sequence, not a promised completion schedule.

## 6. Essential interior coordination brief

Keep current interior studies as references. Before concealing services, coordinate two kitchen appliance schedules, water and waste positions, laundry connections, bathroom fixtures, electrical loads and socket locations, ventilation and tank/pump arrangements. Final cabinetry, finishes, loose furniture and appliance purchasing are later decisions. The ₹3 lakh fixed-interior cap is provisional; loose furniture and appliances need a separate reserve or an agreed reduction elsewhere.
