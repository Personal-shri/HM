# Contractor pricing and design-release package

Prepared for the HM concept near Latur. Status: request template only; no contractor has been contacted and no bid has been obtained. Current enlarged plan and ₹45 lakh compact-redesign target are separate scopes and must not be mixed in one price.

## Information to provide before a comparable fixed quotation

- Selected scope and total floor area; identify phase boundaries if phased.
- Architect's coordinated room, door/window and dimensioned elevation drawings.
- Survey/site levels, access information and geotechnical report.
- Engineer-approved foundation, frame, slab, staircase and balcony drawings.
- Coordinated electrical, plumbing, drainage, waterproofing and service drawings.
- Quantity schedule and finish specifications with explicit brand/model or performance requirements.

## Required quotation columns

Item ID; description; drawing/revision; quantity; unit; material specification; material rate; labour rate; combined rate; amount; taxes; freight/handling; wastage basis; contractor overhead/margin; inclusions; exclusions; validity; lead time; warranty; payment milestone. When a composite rate is used, do not add its material and labour again.

No unmeasured quantity should appear as a confirmed quantity. Use “TBD from drawing” until measured. Allowances and provisional sums must be separately identified.

## Comparison procedure

Ask three contractors to price the same released scope. Normalize exclusions and taxes before comparing totals. Separate alternatives from the base bid. Record quantity changes and reasons. Do not select a cheaper offer by silently accepting weaker waterproofing or unapproved structural changes.

## Quality and release gates

Structural specifications must come from the engineer, not a rendered image or a per-square-foot allocation. Record material approval, sample acceptance, inspection/testing and curing requirements in the contract. Coordinate concealed services before casting or finishing. Obtain the architect/engineer’s acceptance for substitutions affecting performance or geometry.
